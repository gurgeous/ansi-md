| # of colors | Nick        | What?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 16          | 4 bit       | The classic [16 ANSI colors](en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANSI_escape_code#Colors). `Black, Red, Green, Yellow, Blue, Magenta, Cyan & White`, but wily users will setup "themes" to remap these. Your app has zero control over the actual colors, jokes on you! |
| 256         | "colorcube" | Eventually our ansi overlords decided to expand to [256 colors](en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANSI_escape_code#8-bit). This is a huge improvement and you can often build great apps that only use these colors.                                                  |
| 16 mil      | "truecolor" | Yes, full RGB in your terminal. Some apps like to crank it up to 11. I recently wrote a terminal app that shows a short movie on the error page. Truecolor required!                                                                                      |
