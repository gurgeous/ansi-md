Here is a giant table of all the ansi 256 colors and all the tailwind colors. You can compare how the color works as fg/bg with dark and light terminal themes. This is a nice way to pick readlable colors.

Hover any cell to see the APCA contrast between bg and fg. A dot indicates "good contrast", which technically means >= 40. Personally I find those to be readable, though tastes vary and anything between 40 & 50 should be scrutinized.

There are some ambidextrous colors with dots for both light and dark backgrounds. Could be a good choice for lazy people (like me) who want to sidestep terminal theme detection.
